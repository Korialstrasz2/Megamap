MEGAMAP 1.2 / OFFLINE WORKBENCH

One atlas.
Four map modes.

Generate a campaign region or local landscape survey, design a shaped city, and prepare square or hex-grid encounters in the same atlas. Start with a preset, change its seed and settings, then make the result your own.

Build & edit

Generation adds a new map. Select a regional settlement to open a linked city. Shift-click to select multiple objects. Drag selected objects, or their path nodes, to edit them.

Road, river, wall, door and area tools use click-to-place vertices. Press Enter to finish and Escape to cancel. Battle brushes carve floors or fill solid cells.

Save & share

Save atlas downloads your editable project. Autosave is local convenience, not a backup. The Export panel creates SVG, PNG, WebP and Universal VTT files.

Player-safe export omits GM-only objects. Notes and hidden secrets remain in the atlas: never share a GM atlas with players.
